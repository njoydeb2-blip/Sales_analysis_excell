# AtliQ Hardwares: Sales Analysis Portfolio Project 📊

## Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive **Sales Performance Analysis** for **AtliQ Hardwares**, a global electronics manufacturer. Using **Microsoft Excel**, raw sales data was transformed into actionable insights to help the company evaluate **market performance**, **customer growth**, and **product profitability** across different regions and divisions.

The analysis focuses on key business metrics such as:

- **Net Sales**
- **Growth %** (2021 vs 2020)
- **Performance vs. Target**  

for the fiscal years **2019, 2020, and 2021**.

---

## 🚀 Key Reports & Insights

### 1. Customer Performance Report
An in-depth look at how different customers (retailers) contributed to revenue.

- **Top Performer:** Amazon led the way in 2021 with **$82.09M** in net sales, showing **218.9% growth** over 2020.
- **Highest Growth:** Nova saw a staggering **2,664.9% increase** in sales year-over-year.
- **Total Revenue:** The grand total for net sales across all customers reached **$598.88M** in 2021.

### 2. Market Performance vs. Target
Evaluation of how different countries performed against their set sales targets.

- **India:** Largest market with **$161.26M** in sales, missing target by **$9.55M (-5.9%)**.
- **USA:** Second largest market with **$87.78M**, missing target by **$10.24M (-11.7%)**.
- **Global Health:** Overall, the company fell **9.2% short** of its global target, amounting to a **$54.94M deficit**.

### 3. Product Analysis (Top 10 & Bottom 5)
Analysis of products driving growth vs. underperformers.

- **Top Growth Product:** AQ Smash 2 achieved **2,489.5% growth** in sales between 2020 and 2021.
- **Volume Leaders:** AQ Master Wired X1 Ms sold **4.2M units**.
- **Underperformers:** AQ HOME Allin1 Gen 2 had the lowest sales volume at **8.9K units**.

### 4. Division & New Product Performance
Insights into divisions and newly launched products.

- **Division Growth:** PC division showed the highest growth at **313.7%**.
- **New Product Success:** Among 2021 launches, **AQ Qwerty** generated the highest revenue at **$22.0M**.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Skills Demonstrated
- **Data Cleaning & ETL:** Handling large datasets and ensuring consistency across fiscal years.
- **Pivot Tables:** Summarizing millions of rows into readable reports.
- **Financial Modeling:** Calculating **Year-over-Year (YoY) growth** and target variance percentages.
- **Excel Formulas:** Implementing complex logic for net sales and quantity distributions.
